It's not a new observation but it bears repeating. It's very easy to drift if you don't have a clear intent.

This is doesn't mean you need your life tickbox-ed into oblivion. It doesn't mean lock yourself into doing *that one thing* because you definitely want to do more of *that one thing* but...

It does help to give yourself a direction. Even if it's just *a bit more of this* or *a bit less of this*. It's never been easier to waste every waking hour in a staring context with a plastic rectangle that you barely recall the next day.

Oh yeah. I'd like to do a bit more of this.

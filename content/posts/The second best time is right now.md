> "The best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago. The second best is now."

Supposedly this comes from "an old Chinese proverb". Some basic internet digging suggests this came into common usage in the 1960s (as [prompted by someone equally suspicious about putting any vaguely wise-sounding soundbites in the "ancient Chinese wisdom" bucket](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/603690/origins-of-the-phrase-the-best-time-to-plant-a-tree-was-30-years-ago-the-secon) too quickly. 

In any case, the sentiment holds.

I've been meaning to write more for a little while now. And then once you've written a thing why not share it? Not with expectation. Not because everyone desperately needs to hear what I have to say. 

I'm sharing because:
1. It increases the chance that someone might read a thing that I write and share something that makes the next thing better.
2. Someone might take an idea from what I've written and find it helpful or interesting (maybe both?)

I have to credit the written word of [Russell Davies](https://www.russelldavies.com/) - who recently released [Do interesting](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/do-interesting-notice-collect-connect/7426770?ean=9781914168208) & [Giles Turnbull](https://gilest.org/about.html) - author of [The agile comms handbook](https://agilecommshandbook.com/) - as a gentle shove in this direction.

I don't (yet) have a topic or focus to what I'll write here. Let's see what emerges.

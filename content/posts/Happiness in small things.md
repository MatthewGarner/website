title: Happiness in small things

draft: false

Today I got a gift. It made me happy. 

It wasn't a large gift. It didn't fill an impossibly deep hole in my existence. But it made me happy. 

It was socks. It made me happy.

No it isn't Christmas. I play regular football with a group from work and have been trudging out in the same pair of socks that I bought nearly 10 years ago playing 5-a-side at my first job out of university. Understandably, they were showing their age. 

The colours, faded. The material, bobbly, irregular and with more holes than the regulation single hole.

It registered as a minor irritant. An irk. A slight shudder of embarrassment each week as they disappear into the laundry basket and sleep for a few days. 

Hannah noticed. Noticed something she didn't have to. It needn't have registered. When we care, we notice things beyond what is required or asked of us. 

That thought, that made me happy.

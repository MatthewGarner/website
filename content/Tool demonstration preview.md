A team can have enough capacity on average and still keep people waiting. Work arrives unevenly, and some items take longer than others. Spare capacity helps absorb that variation.

```tool
example: flow-queues-v1
caption: Increase demand towards ten items a week. What happens to the time an item spends in the system?
```

The example holds the team, work size and variability fixed. It models one queue, not a forecast for a real team. At or above capacity, there is no stable waiting-time estimate: unfinished work can keep accumulating.

The useful question is how much room the system needs to absorb variation, and what we give up when we remove it.
